Y-Splice Kit

Underwater cable Y-splice kit assembly developed for oceanographic field deployments.

ROLE: Mechanical Design Co-op

DATE: May 2026 – Aug 2026

Background

Underwater cable splice kit product I designed while at Blue Robotics, creating a reliable pot-and-seal method for branching subsea power and data lines.

System Overview

Includes polyurethane potting mold shells, strain relief boots, and reusable clamping hardware designed for field assembly.

Exploded CAD view showing two-part reusable potting mold and cable positioning inserts.

Engineering & CAD Details

Hydrostatic pressure testing performed up to 300m depth rating to verify epoxy resin adhesion and seal integrity over time.
