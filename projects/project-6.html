Vacuum Tester

Portable seal testing device designed during internship at Blue Robotics.

ROLE: Mechanical Design Co-op

DATE: May 2026 – Aug 2026

Background

Handheld automatic leak-detector product I developed while at Blue Robotics to streamline subsea enclosure seal verification prior to ocean deployment.

System Overview

Integrated vacuum pump system, pressure sensor interface, and custom molded enclosure designed for field reliability in harsh marine environments.
