2024 FRC 865 Comp Robot

Team 865's 2024 competition robot on the field during regional matches.

ROLE: Design Lead & Mentor

DATE: Oct 2023 – Jun 2024

Background

Team 865's FIRST Robotics Competition robot I led design of for the 2024 game challenge, overseeing CAD development, subsystem integration, and student fabrication mentorship.

System Overview

High-speed swerve drive chassis, under-bumper intake mechanism, and variable-angle shooter assembly optimized for rapid game-piece scoring cycles.

Complete 3D CAD assembly in Onshape illustrating packaging of intake, shooter, and climber subsystems.

Engineering & CAD Details

Extensive use of CNC sheet aluminum plates, custom 3D printed sensor mounts, and polycarb shielding designed for high-impact match play.

Media Gallery
