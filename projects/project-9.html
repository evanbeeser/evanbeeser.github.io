2024 FRC 865 Comp Robot

Role: Design Lead | Date: Oct 2023 – Jun 2024

Team 865's FIRST Robotics Competition robot I led design of for 2024 game challenge.

Overview

Served as Design Lead for FIRST Robotics Competition Team 865, directing full mechanical architecture, CAD workflows, and fabrication for the 2024 competition robot, winning an official District Excellence in Engineering Award.

Design & Analysis

Managed end-to-end OnShape CAD assembly structures across a multi-subsystem machine. Oversaw rapid prototyping cycles utilizing laser cutting, 3D printing, and CNC router operations to achieve aggressive robot cycle times.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Led mechanical design for high-speed game-piece intake, indexing, and launcher mechanisms.
  • Engineered high-durability aluminum structural frames built to endure high-velocity impacts.
  • Coordinated team fabrication schedules, drawing generation, and BOM tracking throughout build season.
