Architectural Fabrication Drawings

Role: Design Intern | Date: Apr 2025 – Aug 2025

Comprehensive CAD modeling workflows, submittal packages, and production drawing packages for custom architectural metalwork.

Overview

Produced detailed production and architectural submittal drawing packages at Sixpenny Architectural Fabrication for complex custom metalwork installations.

Design & Analysis

Developed parametric OnShape and SolidWorks models for custom staircases, structural railings, and architectural installations. Applied Design for Assembly (DfA) and Poka-yoke principles to simplify shop fabrication and field installation.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Created full structural drawing sets with surface finish specifications and weld symbols.
  • Generated flat-pattern sheet metal layouts optimized for laser cutting and CNC press brakes.
  • Coordinated submittal packages directly with architectural clients and general contractors.
