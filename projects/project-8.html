Underwater Thruster Dynamometer Rig

Role: Mechanical Design Co-Op | Date: May 2026 – Aug 2026

Custom dynamic test bench for measuring thrust output and power efficiency in marine propulsion systems.

Overview

Engineered an underwater thruster dynamometer test rig to measure thrust vectoring, load torque, and electrical efficiency profiles of marine thrusters.

Design & Analysis

Designed corrosion-resistant structural anodized aluminum frames and load cell linkages to isolate thrust forces under wet testing environments. Developed real-time telemetry routines for high-speed dynamic data collection.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Calibrated strain gauge load cells to evaluate static and dynamic thrust response.
  • Integrated high-pot electrical testing and waterproof cable splice enclosures.
  • Mapped motor thermal generation against fluid flow profiles across long run cycles.
