2025 TMR Rover Arm Cycloidal Reducer

Role: Arm System Lead | Date: Sept 2024 – May 2025

Ultra lightweight cycloidal speed reducer for TMR 2025 rover arm shoulder joint.

Overview

Engineered a custom, high-torque-density cycloidal speed reducer specifically designed to serve as the main shoulder actuation drive for the 2025 Toronto MetRobotics rover arm.

Design & Analysis

Calculated custom mathematical cycloid disc profiles to maximize pin contact ratio under heavy dynamic cantilever loads. Conducted FEA stress evaluations on input bearing journals and output drive pins to optimize aluminum component wall thicknesses for weight reduction.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Achieved high gear reduction within a compact, low-profile joint housing envelope.
  • Integrated custom needle roller bearings and precision output shafts to minimize backlash.
  • Validated actuator efficiency, thermal performance, and torque transfer on a dedicated test bench.
