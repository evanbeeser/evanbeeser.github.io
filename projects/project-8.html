2025 TMR Rover Arm Cycloidal Reducer

Completed 2025 cycloidal gearhead mounted inside shoulder joint enclosure.

ROLE: Mechanical Designer

DATE: Sep 2024 – Dec 2024

Background

Ultra lightweight cycloidal speed reducer for TMR 2025 rover arm shoulder joint, engineered to increase joint reduction ratio while minimizing mass.

System Overview

Single-stage cycloidal gearing design incorporating custom aluminum pin rings and high-capacity main cross-roller output bearings.

Tooth profile geometry optimized for continuous multi-pin engagement to minimize stress concentration.

Engineering & CAD Details

Detailed tolerances and clearance fits engineered to accommodate thermal expansion during high-torque holding operations.
