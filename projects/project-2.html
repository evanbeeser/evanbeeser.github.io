6-DOF Robotic Arm System

Role: Arm System Lead | Date: Sept 2024 – Present

Custom 6-degree-of-freedom manipulator designed for planetary rover payload manipulation.

Overview

Led the full mechanical lifecycle of a custom 6-degree-of-freedom robotic arm engineered for high reach-to-weight ratio, precise positioning, and dynamic payload handling under harsh competition conditions.

Design & Analysis

Executed comprehensive kinematic modeling and joint trajectory generation in MATLAB to establish reachable workspace boundaries. Applied structural FEA to optimize aluminum link cross-sections for mass reduction while maintaining high torsional rigidity across all dynamic poses.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Designed lightweight modular joint housings incorporating integrated harmonic and cycloidal reducers.
  • Utilized Poka-yoke assembly practices and integrated internal cable routing through joint pivots.
  • Managed production drawing packages featuring complete GD&T callouts and fits.
