2026 TMR Rover Arm

Role: Arm System Lead | Date: Sept 2025 – Present

All new 6-DoF robotic manipulator arm designed for TMR 2026 URC competition.

Overview

Led the full mechanical design and development of an all-new 6-degree-of-freedom manipulator arm for the Toronto MetRobotics (TMR) rover, engineered specifically for University Rover Challenge (URC) competition tasks.

Design & Analysis

Executed dynamic trajectory and kinematic modeling in MATLAB to optimize joint range and workspace coverage. Performed finite element analysis (FEA) on structural links to maximize strength-to-weight ratio while maintaining strict deflection bounds under maximum payload leverage.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Designed modular joint assemblies integrating high-reduction custom actuators.
  • Implemented internal cable management to protect signal and power harnesses during full joint rotations.
  • Engineered end-effector mounting interfaces for seamless task-specific tool swaps.
