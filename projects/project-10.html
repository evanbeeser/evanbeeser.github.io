2023 FRC 865 Comp Robot

Role: Mechanism Lead | Date: Oct 2022 – Jun 2023

For 865's 2023 robot I designed our two game-piece end effector.

Overview

Designed and implemented the dual game-piece end effector mechanism for FRC Team 865's 2023 competition robot, engineered to manipulate both cones and cubes with high precision.

Design & Analysis

Developed compliance geometry and high-friction roller layouts to secure varied game element shapes securely under rapid arm accelerations. Utilized lightweight carbon fiber and 3D printed components to minimize end-of-arm mass.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Engineered active motor-driven intake rollers with custom pneumatic clamping actuation.
  • Optimized weight distribution to reduce dynamic motor load on the main arm linkages.
  • Prototyped and field-tested multiple jaw geometries during off-season testing.
