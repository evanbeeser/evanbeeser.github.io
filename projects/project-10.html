FRC Competition Robot Mechanical Lead

Role: Design Lead (Team 865) | Date: Oct 2020 – Jun 2024

Award-winning high-speed competition robot designed and manufactured for FIRST Robotics Competition.

Overview

Directed mechanical design and CAD development for FIRST Robotics Competition Team 865 over multi-year robot build cycles, earning an official District Event Excellence in Engineering Award.

Design & Analysis

Led team CAD workflows in OnShape, designing swerve drive chassis assemblies, game-piece intake mechanisms, and pneumatic elevation systems. Optimized rapid prototyping iteration cycles utilizing in-house FDM 3D printing and router machining.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Spearheaded full CAD assembly management and bill of materials (BOM) tracking.
  • Implemented robust sheet metal and tubular frame structures capable of high-impact collisions.
  • Mentored junior team members in 3D modeling, machining, and mechanical assembly techniques.
