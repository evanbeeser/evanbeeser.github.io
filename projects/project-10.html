2023 FRC 865 Comp Robot

Team 865's 2023 competition robot scoring game pieces on the grid.

ROLE: Design Lead & Mentor

DATE: Oct 2022 – Jun 2023

Background

For 865's 2023 robot I designed our two game-piece end effector capable of reliably picking and scoring both cones and inflatable cubes.

System Overview

Compliant roller claw design featuring pneumatic actuation and current-sensing motor feedback for auto-securing game elements.

Detailed CAD view of the dual-action end-effector mechanism and roller geometry.

Engineering & CAD Details

Optimized carbon fiber tube structural supports to maintain end-effector stiffness while reducing mass at the end of the telescoping arm.
