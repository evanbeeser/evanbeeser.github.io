Cycloidal Actuator Development

High-torque, compact speed reduction for custom robotic arm joints.

Arm System Lead | Sept 2024 – Present

Overview

Designed and manufactured a custom low-backlash cycloidal speed reducer tailored for high-torque robotic joints. The goal was to maximize power density while minimizing overall mass and axial length.

Design & Analysis

Generated custom cycloidal rotor profiles in CAD to optimize pin engagement and reduce stress concentrations under peak dynamic loads. Evaluated profile geometry using parametric modeling tools and performed static stress analyses on key torque-transmitting components.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Modeled dual-disc cycloidal gear geometry to eliminate eccentric vibrations.
  • Integrated custom needle roller pins and precision output shaft bearings.
  • Validated back-drivability and positional accuracy on a test bench setup.
