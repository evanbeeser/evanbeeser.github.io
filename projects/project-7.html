Sixpenny Corten Cladding

Role: Design Intern | Date: Apr 2025 – Aug 2025

Welded corten steel cladding system I helped design while at Sixpenny Architectural Fabrication.

Overview

Assisted in the end-to-end mechanical design, sheet metal layout, and detailing of a custom welded corten steel exterior cladding system during my design internship at Sixpenny Architectural Fabrication.

Design & Analysis

Generated detailed CAD models and flat-pattern layouts in OnShape, factoring in material bend allowances, laser-cutting kerf, and structural thermal expansion. Integrated hidden fast-assembly bracketry to ensure a seamless exterior aesthetic.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Drafted comprehensive shop drawing packages with weld symbols and surface weathering guidelines.
  • Utilized Design for Assembly (DfA) principles to reduce site installation time for field technicians.
  • Coordinated fabrication specifications directly with brake press operators and welders.
