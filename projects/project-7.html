Automated Seal Testing Device

Role: Mechanical Design Co-Op | Date: May 2026 – Aug 2026

Portable handheld pressure cycling fixture for rapid underwater enclosure seal verification.

Overview

Designed and validated a portable, handheld seal-testing device during an engineering co-op at Blue Robotics to accelerate Quality Assurance workflows for subsea enclosure products.

Design & Analysis

Developed rapid pneumatics and pressure cycling systems to automate seal integrity qualification. Constructed an ergonomic enclosure using functional 3D printing and custom machined sealing interfaces.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Automated pressure decay testing cycles to reduce manual QA duration per unit.
  • Selected subsea-grade O-ring seals and designed custom sealing gland geometries.
  • Executed hyperbaric pressure testing to ensure physical test rig durability.
