Custom Harmonic Actuator Integration

Role: Mechanical Designer | Date: Sept 2024 – Present

Zero-backlash strain wave gearing integration for high-precision robot joint control.

Overview

Developed custom harmonic actuator drive assemblies to provide zero-backlash motion control in high-precision robot arm wrist and elbow joints.

Design & Analysis

Engineered custom flexspline and wave generator mounting interfaces using SolidWorks. Performed precise tolerance stack-up evaluations to maintain strict radial concentricity and pre-load requirements across high-power density drive components.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Integrated cross-roller bearings to support high combined axial and moment loading.
  • Configured absolute position encoders and hall sensors with ODrive controllers.
  • Fabricated custom aluminum mounting plates utilizing precision CNC milling workflows.
