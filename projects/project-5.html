REX

REX combat robot fully assembled in its final competition configuration for BotBrawl.

ROLE: Personal Design Project

DATE: 2024 – 2025

Background

A 3lb beetleweight combat robot built for the local Ontario BotBrawl league as a personal project, testing high-energy weapon drive integration and impact-resistant chassis designs.

System Overview

Custom 6061-T6 aluminum frame, UHMW top armor, and high-RPM brushless weapon drive assembly engineered to withstand severe shock loads.

Internal component layout detailing ESC placement, battery retention, and drive motors.

Engineering & CAD Details

CAD modeling focused on weight distribution to keep the center of mass balanced directly over the drive wheels for maximum traction.
