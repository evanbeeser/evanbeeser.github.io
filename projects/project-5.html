REX

Role: Designer & Fabricator | Date: Personal Project

A 3lb beetleweight combat robot built for the local Ontario BotBrawl league as a personal project.

Overview

Designed and constructed REX, a custom 3lb beetleweight combat robot engineered from the ground up for high-impact durability and maneuverability in the local Ontario BotBrawl competition circuit.

Design & Analysis

Selected high-strength shock-absorbing polymers and aluminum structural framing to withstand massive impact forces. Calculated weapon energy storage and motor torque curves to maximize kinetic energy delivery while remaining strictly within the 3lb weight limit.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Modeled full assembly in CAD with optimized mass distribution for low center of gravity.
  • Fabricated impact-resistant unibody armor plates using custom machining and 3D printing techniques.
  • Wired and integrated high-discharge LiPo power systems, ESCs, and receiver electronics.
